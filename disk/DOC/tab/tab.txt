TAB---Tab is an OSK program that will tabulate 6809 and 68000assembly language source.* Opcode-aware so that it knows when arguments are present* Will tabulate code that will not assemble* Indenting visually unscrambles deeply-nested conditionals.* Form feeds inserted for the pag pseudo-op.* Selectable field widths* Simple conditional-match checking* Operates as a filterYou may use and distribute tab freely.
