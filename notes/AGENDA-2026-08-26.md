## 2. Work the programs that do not run

The sweep in §1 produces the list, which is why it goes first. This is the

## 3. Audit the silent cio.l + clib.l collision

Today's finding, and the part worth generalising: `cc -qixm` hands l68 both
`cio.l` and this SDK's full `clib.l`. For thirteen of 290 programs that is a
LOUD `caused name clashes` and the driver steps down. For `card` it was
SILENT -- it linked, ran with two stdio implementations, and took a bus error
forty moves in.

Nothing built by the driver is installed today, so this is latent rather than
shipped. But it means **a clean link is not evidence**. A detector is
straightforward: build each recipe both `-qixm` and `-qm`, run both, compare.
Where they differ, the `-qixm` build is suspect. Long, mechanical, overnight
work, and it either finds more `card`s or retires the worry with a number.
