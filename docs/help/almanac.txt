$ almanac
Syntax of command:
    "almanac [<longitude> <latitude>] <date> <time> [<timezone>]"
where
    "<longitude>" is local longitude (DDD.MM,+W,-E)
    "<latitude>" is local latitude (DD.MM,+N,-S)
    "<date>" is calendar date (MMDD.YYYY)
    "<time>" is time (24 hour format,HH.MMSS)
    "<timezone>" is one of the following: "EST", "CST", "MST", or "PST"

Azimuth and altitude is not computed if the parameter "<longitude>" and
"<latitude>" was omitted.  Universal time is assumed unless a "<timezone>"
flag was supplied.  For the summer months, enter your time minus 1 hour if
daylight saving time is in effect in your local time zone.

All displayed angles are in degrees, minutes, seconds (if displayed) format
except for right ascension which is in hours, minutes, seconds format.
Azimuth is measured clockwise from north in the plane of the horizon.
