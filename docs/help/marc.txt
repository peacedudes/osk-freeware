MARC - archive merger, Version 5.21, created on 04/22/87 at 15:05:10
Usage: MARC <tgtarc> <srcarc> [<filename> . . .]
Where: <tgtarc> is the archive to add files to,
       <srcarc> is the archive to get files from, and
       <filename> is zero or more file names to get.
