$ sbatch -?
Syntax: sbatch [<opts>] <system> [<opts>]
Function: sends spoolt newsarticle to <system>
Options:
     -c      Send articles compressed
     -c7     Send articles compressed and encoded
     -b      Send articles batched (default)
     -s      Send articles as single files
     -f=<nr> sets the maximum spoolfile size to <nr> kByte
