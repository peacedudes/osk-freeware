$ chd /dd/CMDS; ksh man
man -- the librarian for this disk

  man <name>       everything about a program, a document at a time
  man -k <word>    which programs are about <word>   (man -k chess)
  man -f <name>    what a program is, in one line
  man -w <name>    where its documents and source are
  man -s <name>    read its source

Reading, space pages on, b pages back, /word searches, :n goes to
the next document and :p comes back.  q leaves.
