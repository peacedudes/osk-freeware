$ weekday -h
Usage: weekday [-svdDmMywjnah] [<date>]
   <date>: mm/dd/yy  |  dd-mm-yy  |  yyyy-mm-dd
  |           yy.ddd  |  Unix date
   default is today
   options: -s toggle short form output
            -v toggle verbose output
            -d prints day of week number
            -D gives day of week name
            -m prints the month number
            -M outputs the month name
            -y outputs the year number
            -w gives the week number
            -j outputs the julian day number
            -n gives day of month
            -a gives everything it knows (default)
            -h give a usage message
