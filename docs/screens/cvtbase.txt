$ cvtbase
syntax: cvtbase <input base> <output base>
function: convert numbers in <stdin> from one base to another.
options: (none)
<base> is specified by one of the following:
        b       binary
        d       decimal
        h or x  hexadecimal
        o       octal
        or the actual digit characters (eg. 012 would be ternary notation).
These may be preceded by a - sign to indicate the base is negative.
A base may be preceded by m<char>, to use a different minus sign char.
Bases -1, 0, and 1 are disallowed.
