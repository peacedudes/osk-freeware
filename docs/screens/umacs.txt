/* keep, drop, kept -- take programs off this collection onto your own disk.
 *
 * Written for the OS-9 freeware collection, 2026-08-21.  Microware C (K&R):
 * no prototypes, no const, no ANSI library beyond what OS-9 v2.4 ships.
 *
 * WHY THIS EXISTS, which is the whole design
 *
 * A 900-program disk offers everyone the same bad choice: take all of it, or
 * work out for yourself which pieces you want and how to get them.  The fix
 * is not a better installer.  It is not an installer at all -- "install"
 * means all-or-nothing, unknown extent, and no way back, which is the dread
 * itself.  The verb is `keep': you run things in place from the collection,
 * and when one earns its place you keep it.  Test drive, then take.
 *
 * WHO IT IS FOR
 *
 * Not everybody.  If you mount this collection as /dd -- the recommended
 * arrangement, see DOC/README-RUNNING -- then nothing needs keeping, because
 * everything is already where it expects to be.  `keep' is for the person who
 * ALREADY HAS an OS-9 system on /dd and wants some of this collection on it.
 * For them /dd is theirs and the collection is another drive, so a program
 * that reads /dd/GAMES/adv/glorkz will look on their disk and not find it.
