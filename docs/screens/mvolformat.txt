Syntax: mvolformat drive volname volcount [format options]
Function: format multiple floppies with sequential volume #s
Parameters:
        drive           Drive name (i.e. /d0)
        volname         Base volume name. 'Volnn' gets appended (nn = vol num)
        volcount                Volume count. 1-99
        format options  Any options legal for format, except -r and -v
