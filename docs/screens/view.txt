README-CIO -- the starred programs, and the five modules that serve them
=======================================================================

Some of the names in DOC/INDEX carry a star.  A star means the program needs
one of Microware's runtime trap modules: `cio` for nearly all of them, and
for some `math`, `math881`, `csl` or `csl020`.

THEY RUN HERE.  All five modules are on this disk, in CMDS, included with
Microware's permission -- SOURCES.txt records the exchange.  You need do
nothing, supply nothing, and fetch nothing.  The star tells you what a
program depends on, not what it lacks.

This file explains what the star means, and what to do in the two cases
where you might want a different arrangement.

Why so many programs need it
----------------------------

`cc -qixm` -- the C library in a shared trap handler rather than linked into
every binary -- was the normal way to build for OS-9, and it is why a starred
program is often a fraction of the size of a trap-free one.  Much of this
