$ zot
syntax: zot [<opts>] {<text> [<opts>]}
Function: echo text line(s) to stdout in interesting ways
Options:
        -d          display text line in all styles, one at a time
        -s          help with styles  :-)
        -s[=]<num>  use style <num>
        -z[=<file>] get text from <file> (or stdin)

By: Marc Kriguer (kriguer@ernie.berkeley.edu) and
    Roger Murray (ucla-an!remsit!rem@ee.ucla.edu)

error: either <text> or -z must be specified
