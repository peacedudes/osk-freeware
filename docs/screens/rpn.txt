WELCOME TO RPN, a reverse polish notation calculator.
the functions are as follows:
add - add the top two numbers on the stack
sub - subtract the top two numbers on the stack
mul - multiply the top two numbers on the stack
div - divide the top two numbers on the stack
pop - discard the top element of the stack
mod - take the remainder of the division of the top two numbers on the stack
not - take the compliment of the top element on the stack
help - display this help
xor - exclusive or
or - not-exclusive or
and - bitwise and
pr [number] - print the number [number] deep in the stack. defaults to 0
sw - switch printing top of stack on/off
(not that you'll ever need this, but:)
end - terminate the progran
