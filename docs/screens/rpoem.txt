$ rpoem 3
------------------------------------------
Verse 1
A lustful shovel should think within the taste of a bone
And strengthen time's notion.
But a timorous drop heals a wanton muffin
While the taste of a bone strengthens the lustful shovel.
------------------------------------------
Verse 2
A gray petal should aspire of a bone of night
And arouse the movement of a rake.
But beauty's bounty becomes a gray lark
While a bone of night kills the gray petal.
------------------------------------------
Verse 3
A rusty muffin may aspire on a twig of age
And revere the spoon of the action of a trap.
But a happy rake pursues the flower beside faith over love
While a twig of age reveres the rusty muffin.
------------------------------------------
