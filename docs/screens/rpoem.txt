$ rpoem 3
------------------------------------------
Verse 1
A frail lark must gurgle under the motion of a wagon wheel
And worship a pensive petal.
But a pensive dove pursues power of time
While the motion of a wagon wheel reveres the frail lark.
------------------------------------------
Verse 2
A timid raven should dance under the tooth up a timorous book
And becalm a wagon wheel of night.
But love over time pursues a happy face
While the tooth up a timorous book worships the timid raven.
------------------------------------------
Verse 3
A frolicking shovel should bask on strength of night
And strengthen the age of a star.
But peace on wisdom understands nature's motion
While strength of night becalms the frolicking shovel.
------------------------------------------
