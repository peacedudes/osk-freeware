     Long ago in a small village there was a little
girl dressed in red who went to a super market and bought
a cat. On the way home they came upon a fence which
the cat was afraid to cross. The little girl dressed
in red said "cat, cat, jump over the fence or I won't
get home tonight."  But the cat would not.  The little
girl dressed in red went over hill and dale and she
met a smoke.  She said, "smoke, smoke, suffocate cat,
cat won't jump over the fence and I shan't get home
tonight."  But the smoke would not.  The little girl
dressed in red went down the path and she met a wind.
She said, "wind, wind, disperse smoke,  smoke won't
suffocate the cat,   cat won't jump over the fence and
I shan't get home tonight."  But the wind would not.
The little girl dressed in red went near the black mill
and she met a smoke.  She said, "smoke, smoke, pollute
wind,  wind won't disperse the smoke,  smoke won't suffocate
the cat,   cat won't jump over the fence and I shan't
get home tonight."  But the smoke would not.  The little
girl dressed in red went down the path and she met a
wind.  She said, "wind, wind, disperse smoke,  smoke
won't pollute the wind,  wind won't disperse the smoke,
smoke won't suffocate the cat,   cat won't jump over
the fence and I shan't get home tonight."  But the wind
would not.  The little girl dressed in red went down
the path and she met a smoke.  She said, "smoke, smoke,
pollute wind,  wind won't disperse the smoke,  smoke
won't pollute the wind,  wind won't disperse the smoke,
smoke won't suffocate the cat,   cat won't jump over
the fence and I shan't get home tonight."  The smoke
knew the little girl dressed in red and, in fact, had
been saved by her from a wild lion.  So the  smoke began
to pollute the wind; the  wind began to disperse the
smoke; the  smoke began to pollute the wind; the  wind
began to disperse the smoke; the  smoke began to suffocate
the cat; the   cat began to jump over the fence and
the little girl dressed in red got home that night.
