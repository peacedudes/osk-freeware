tonight."  But the wind would not.  The kind old aunt
went on the road to the green house and she met a smoke.
She said, "smoke, smoke, pollute wind,  wind won't disperse
the smoke,  smoke won't pollute the wind,  wind won't
blow-out the fire,  fire won't burn the parrot,   parrot
won't jump over the fence and I shan't get home tonight."
The smoke knew the kind old aunt and, in fact, had been
saved by her from a wild camel.  So the  smoke began
to pollute the wind; the  wind began to disperse the
smoke; the  smoke began to pollute the wind; the  wind
began to blow-out the fire; the  fire began to burn
the parrot; the   parrot began to jump over the fence
and the kind old aunt got home that night.
