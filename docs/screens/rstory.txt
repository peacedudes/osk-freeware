won't sting the farmer,  farmer won't break the knife,
knife won't cut the cat,   cat won't jump over the fence
and I shan't get home tonight."  But the fire would
not.  The little girl dressed in red went down the path
and she met a wind.  She said, "wind, wind, blow-out
fire,  fire won't burn the hunter,  hunter won't swat
the bee,  bee won't sting the farmer,  farmer won't
break the knife,  knife won't cut the cat,   cat won't
jump over the fence and I shan't get home tonight."
The wind knew the little girl dressed in red and, in
fact, had been saved by her from a wild giraffe.  So
the  wind began to blow-out the fire; the  fire began
to burn the hunter; the  hunter began to swat the bee;
the  bee began to sting the farmer; the  farmer began
to break the knife; the  knife began to cut the cat;
the   cat began to jump over the fence and the little
girl dressed in red got home that night.
