the wind would not.  The nice old lady went by the yellow
house and then went over a hill and she met a smoke.
She said, "smoke, smoke, pollute wind,  wind won't disperse
the smoke,  smoke won't pollute the wind,  wind won't
blow-out the fire,  fire won't burn the dog,   dog won't
jump over the fence and I shan't get home tonight."
The smoke knew the nice old lady and, in fact, had been
saved by her from a wild ostrich.  So the  smoke began
to pollute the wind; the  wind began to disperse the
smoke; the  smoke began to pollute the wind; the  wind
began to blow-out the fire; the  fire began to burn
the dog; the   dog began to jump over the fence and
the nice old lady got home that night.
