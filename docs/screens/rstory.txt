The nice old lady went over a hill and she met a smoke.
She said, "smoke, smoke, suffocate girl,  girl won't
spill the liquor,  liquor won't intoxicate the hunter,
hunter won't swat the bee,  bee won't sting the cat,
cat won't jump over the fallen tree and I shan't get
home tonight."  The smoke knew the nice old lady and,
in fact, had been saved by her from a wild lion.  So
the  smoke began to suffocate the girl; the  girl began
to spill the liquor; the  liquor began to intoxicate
the hunter; the  hunter began to swat the bee; the
bee began to sting the cat; the   cat began to jump
over the fallen tree and the nice old lady got home
that night.
