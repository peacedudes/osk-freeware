tonight."  But the wind would not.  The little old woman
went toward the dark school and she met a smoke.  She
said, "smoke, smoke, pollute wind,  wind won't disperse
the smoke,  smoke won't suffocate the farmer,  farmer
won't break the sword,  sword won't cut the parrot,
parrot won't jump over the large rock and I shan't get
home tonight."  The smoke knew the little old woman
and, in fact, had been saved by her from a wild tiger.
So the  smoke began to pollute the wind; the  wind began
to disperse the smoke; the  smoke began to suffocate
the farmer; the  farmer began to break the sword; the
sword began to cut the parrot; the   parrot began to
jump over the large rock and the little old woman got
home that night.
