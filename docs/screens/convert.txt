You are standing on a steeply climbing trail nearing the top of
a pass. The trail is ill-defined here, obviously the work of
some alien animal. To the south, far below, you can barely make
out the site hacked out of the virgin forest by your landing
party. The only objects visible are the main landing craft which
brought you to this strange world, and the huge transparent
geodesic dome which still houses the rest of your friends.
Even here, only a few tens of meters from the crest of the
trail, the suns are shining brightly, totally unobscured by the
odd mirage effect which so far has prevented your mother ship
850 kilometers above from getting clear photos of the large
valley which must lie just beyond the pass you now approach.
