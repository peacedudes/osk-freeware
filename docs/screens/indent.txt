/*
 * about -- everything this collection knows about one program, on one
 * screen.
 *
 * Written for the OS-9 freeware collection, 2026-08-22. Microware C (K&R).
 *
 * WHY
 *
 * The disk already knows, for every program, what it is (DOC/INDEX), what it is
 * FOR (DOC/CATEGORIES), where it came from (DOC/ORIGINS), what files it
 * opens (DOC/DEPENDS), whether its source survived (SRC/) and whether anyone
 * documented it (DOC/<name>/). All of that is here, on the disk, in plain
 * text -- and to use it you had to know those five places existed and go and
 * read each one.
 *
 * That is a card catalogue with no cards. This prints the card:
 *
 * $ about fortune fortune        print a random quotation
 *
 * for        Games -- Fortune & sayings from       usenet archive  fortune.ar
