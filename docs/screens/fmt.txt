FIRST: YOU MAY NOT NEED THIS AT ALL -----------------------------------

If you mount this collection as /dd -- the recommended arrangement, and
what DOC/README-RUNNING is about -- then nothing needs keeping.  Every
program is already where it expects to be, and 258 of them read data
from /dd that is right here.  Run them in place and be done.

`keep' is for the other case: you ALREADY RUN OS-9, /dd is YOUR system,
and this collection is mounted alongside as another drive.  Then a
program that reads /dd/GAMES/adv/glorkz will look on your disk and not
find it.  `keep' is what puts it there -- the program, and the files it
reads, in the places it looks for them.

That is the whole job: make a program that expects the collection at /dd
work on a /dd that is not the collection.
