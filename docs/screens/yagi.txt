$ yagi
This program designs UHF/VHF yagi antennas based on designs by DL6WU.
See "More Gain with Yagi Antennas", VHF Communications, 3/77, 4/77, 3/82

Center frequency of antenna (in MHz)? 144
Number of antenna elements (9-40)? 9
Boom diameter (in millimeters)? 20
Are the elements insulated from the boom (Y/N)? N
You may use any of the following tubing sizes for the elements.
Select the size closest to the commercial tube size.

    1:  6.2mm
    2:  7.3mm
    3:  8.7mm
    4: 10.4mm
    5: 11.7mm
    6: 13.1mm

Tubing size (1-6)? 4
