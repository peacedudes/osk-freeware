$ about wump
 wump           hunt the Wumpus through a cave of tunnels by the hazards you
                sense: a draft means a pit is next door, a smell the Wumpus
                himself.  Move room to room, then loose a crooked arrow along
                a path of rooms to kill him -- but miss and he may wake and eat
                you.  `-h' for a harder cave; `-r'/`-t'/`-a' resize it

  for        GAMES -- Other games
  from       4.3BSD Net/2     usr/src/games/wump, 1990 -- University of California, Berkeley
  source     SRC/wump

  run it     wump
  take it    keep wump
