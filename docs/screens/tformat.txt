FIRST: YOU MAY NOT NEED THIS AT ALL -----------------------------------
If  you  mount  this collection as /dd -- the recommended arrangement, and what
DOC/README-RUNNING is about -- then nothing needs keeping.   Every  program  is
already where it expects to be, and 258 of them read data from /dd that is righ
here.  Run them in place and be done.

collection  is  mounted  alongside as another drive.  Then a program that reads
/dd/GAMES/adv/glorkz will look on your disk and not find it.   `keep'  is  what
